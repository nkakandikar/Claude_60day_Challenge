## Company Overview

IRCTC is a **Navratna PSU** under the Ministry of Railways and the *only* entity authorized to sell online rail tickets, run train catering, and produce packaged drinking water (Rail Neer) across India. In July 2024 it became a Scheduled 'A' Public Sector Undertaking of the Government of India.

**Four business segments:**

| Segment | Role |
|---|---|
| Internet Ticketing | The cash engine — bulk of profit, convenience-fee driven |
| Catering | Mobile, e-catering, and static (food plazas, base kitchens) |
| Rail Neer | Branded packaged drinking water (~19 plants) |
| Tourism | Rail tour packages, lounges, retiring rooms |

## Strengths

1. **Regulated monopoly** with structural pricing power.
2. **Exceptional ROE/ROCE** and a near-zero-debt balance sheet.
3. **Consistent dividend** payout of 49–52%.

## Watch-points

1. **Convenience-fee dependence** — ticketing drives most profit; any government cap or rollback (precedent exists) hits earnings directly.
2. **FII exit** — holding fell to 4.86% in Mar '26 from ~7.2%. The State both owns (62.4%) and regulates the company.

---

## Fundamental Quality: **Strong**

Monopoly economics, pristine balance sheet, high returns. The debate is about **price, not business quality**.
